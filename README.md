## Background

15+ years shipping production software on backend foundations — SaaS platforms, real-time systems, cross-platform applications, Web3 — owning architectural decisions end to end. At Game7 (Nov 2023 – Mar 2026) I designed multi-agent AI content generation with intelligent model routing, built a custom evaluation framework for non-deterministic outputs, and established the team's AI-augmented development workflow. Independent since 2026. Four years working with AI every day.

## How I Work

I design systems where agents and humans build side by side: structured specs as implementation briefs, mandatory non-author review of agent-written code, strict tech-debt and context discipline. Every line shipped is fully understood by the human who owns it — fifteen years of engineering judgment is what makes the AI part work. I direct agents rather than defer to them.

## What I'm Building

Atta Labs is my lab — my own agent teams written as specifications and compiled into executable flows, a multi-LLM engine routing across Claude, Gemini, GPT and Grok with cost/quality/fallback logic, and my own MCP server with bearer auth and envelope-encrypted keys. It's all open — read the code, and read the issues.
